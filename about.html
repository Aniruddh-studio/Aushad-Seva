Mini-Project: AushadSeva

Introduction

  • JanAushadi is an initiative by the Government of India to provide quality medicine at affordable rates.

  • This initiative is implemented under the Ministry of Pharmaceuticals and Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers.

  • Medicines sold under this scheme are from certified pharmaceutical companies and are tested for quality to ensure global standards.

  • Medicines are available at Pradhanmantri JanAushadhi Kendra which are setup at various locations around the country.

  • The government conducts campaigns to create awareness about benefits of generic medicine and this scheme.

  • The objective of this scheme is to promote the use of generic medicine and also encourage availability of high quality generic medicine.

Conclusion

The JanAushadi Yojana is a transformative initiative that aims to address the critical issue of affordable healthcare in our nation.

By promoting accessible medicines, the government is striving to improve the health outcomes for millions of people across the country while ensuring that cost does not become a barrier to healthcare.

The success of this scheme has revolutionized the Indian healthcare system and strives to achieve fair treatment.
